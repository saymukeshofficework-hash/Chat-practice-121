### Question 1

Why does a wet cloth dry faster in moving air?

Moving air carries water vapour away from the cloth, allowing evaporation to continue faster.

> **Answer.** Higher air movement increases evaporation.

**Common mistake.** Evaporation can occur below the boiling point.
