### Question 1

Write a monic quadratic whose zeroes are $4$ and $-3$.

1. The desired factors are $x-4$ and $x+3$.
2. Multiply and collect like terms.

> **Answer.** $x^2-x-12$.

**Common mistake.** The product of the zeroes is negative.
