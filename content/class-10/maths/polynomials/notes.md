## Degree

The degree is the greatest exponent with a nonzero coefficient. For $5x^3-2x+9$, the degree is $3$.

## Zeroes

A number is a zero if substituting it gives $0$. For example, $p(x)=x-9$ has zero $9$.

## Quadratic formula box

> For $ax^2+bx+c$, with $a\ne0$, sum of zeroes $=-b/a$ and product $=c/a$.

## Mind map

- Polynomial → degree → leading coefficient
- Polynomial → zeroes → substitution or factorisation
