### Question 1

Find the sum and product of the zeroes of $2x^2-7x+3$.

1. Read off $a=2$, $b=-7$ and $c=3$.
2. Compute $-b/a=7/2$ and $c/a=3/2$.

> **Answer.** The sum is $7/2$ and the product is $3/2$.

**Common mistake.** Divide both results by the leading coefficient.
