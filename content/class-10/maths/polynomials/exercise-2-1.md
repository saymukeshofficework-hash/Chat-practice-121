### Question 1

The polynomial $p(x)=x^2-5x+6$ has which zeroes?

1. Find two numbers with product $6$ and sum $-5$. They are $-2$ and $-3$.
2. Write $p(x)=(x-2)(x-3)$.
3. Set each factor equal to zero.

> **Answer.** $x=2$ and $x=3$.

**Common mistake.** A factor of $x-2$ gives the zero $2$.

### Question 2

Does $-1$ make $q(x)=x^2+4x+3$ equal to zero?

1. Substitute $x=-1$.
2. Simplify $1-4+3=0$.

> **Answer.** Yes, $-1$ is a zero.

**Common mistake.** Keep the brackets around the negative number when squaring.
