### Question 1 · 2 marks

Find both zeroes of $x^2-4x-5$.

### Question 2 · 2 marks

Find the highest common factor of $45$ and $75$ by prime factorisation.

## Answers

1. $5$ and $-1$, from $(x-5)(x+1)$.
2. $15$, from the shared prime factors $3$ and $5$.
